How we cover the metro

One team, many neighborhoods, each with its own plumbing.

A plumber in Waldo deals with different houses than a plumber in Smithville. Older brick homes near the Plaza have plaster walls and cast iron stacks. Lake properties in the Northland have long water lines and seasonal use. Newer subdivisions in Lee's Summit and Blue Springs have big households and gas-hungry outdoor kitchens.

Every area page below explains what we see most often in that part of the metro, how our six core services apply there, and the questions homeowners ask us. If your neighborhood is not listed, call anyway. We cover more ground than any single page can name.
