Common signs of a failing sewer line

A sewer line rarely fails without warning. It usually announces itself for weeks or months first. Catching it at the symptom stage is the difference between a spot repair and a full replacement.

  • ✓ Repeated backups in the lowest drain in the house, even after the line was cabled.
  • ✓ Sewage odor in the yard, basement, or crawlspace.
  • ✓ A strip of grass along the sewer run that is greener or grows faster than the rest of the lawn.
  • ✓ Soggy ground or a soft depression in the yard with no rain to explain it.
  • ✓ Several fixtures draining slowly at the same time.
  • ✓ Toilets that gurgle after a line has already been cleared once.
  • ✓ New settling or cracking in a slab or foundation near the sewer path.
  • ✓ Rodent or insect activity around a basement floor drain.

Our process and equipment

Sewer work is expensive to guess at. Every recommendation we make starts with camera evidence, not a hunch about the age of the pipe.

Camera inspection first

We run a camera through the line and show you what is actually there: roots, a belly holding water, offset joints, scale, or a collapse. You see the same footage we do.

Locate and mark depth

We locate the line from the surface and mark depth before anyone talks about digging. That determines whether the repair is a two-foot access pit or a full trench.

Trenchless where the host pipe allows

When the existing pipe still has enough structure, trenchless options can restore the line with far less disruption to a finished yard or driveway.

Excavation and spot repair

Where a section has collapsed or separated, we excavate that section rather than replacing the entire run when the rest of the line checks out.

Kansas City ground conditions

Clay soil, freeze-thaw movement, and mature trees put real stress on buried lines here. We account for soil and root pressure in the repair rather than just patching the symptom.

Backfill and verification

We compact the backfill, restore the surface, and re-camera the line so you have proof the repair holds before we close it out.

Frequently asked questions

What affects the cost of sewer line repair?

Depth, length, access, and method. A shallow break under an open lawn is a different job from a deep line under a driveway or mature landscaping. A spot repair on one failed section costs far less than replacing a full run. Trenchless can reduce restoration cost when the host pipe qualifies. The camera inspection is what tells us which of those you are actually looking at, which is why we never quote sewer work blind.

How quickly can you get to my house in the Kansas City metro?

We are a local crew rather than a national dispatch center, so the honest answer depends on where you are and what is already on the schedule. Closer-in areas like Downtown, Midtown, North Kansas City, and Raytown are usually quickest. Independence, Blue Springs, and Lee's Summit take longer, especially in traffic. Call and we will give you a real arrival window instead of a guess.

Do you guarantee sewer line work?

We offer workmanship and parts warranty options, and we work with home warranty programs. What applies depends on the job and the condition of the system, so we tell you which coverage fits before the work starts, not after.

Is a sewer backup an emergency?

Yes. We answer the phone 24 hours a day, seven days a week, holidays included. Plumbing failures do not wait for business hours. If water or sewage is actively coming into the house, call now rather than waiting for morning.
