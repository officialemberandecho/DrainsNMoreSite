Common signs your water heater needs attention

Water heaters usually give warning before they quit or leak. Most of the failures we get called out for on a Sunday night showed at least one of these signs weeks earlier.

  • ✓ No hot water at all, or hot water that runs out much faster than it used to.
  • ✓ Rusty, brown, or metallic-tasting water from the hot side only.
  • ✓ Popping, rumbling, or knocking from the tank when it heats.
  • ✓ Water pooling around the base of the unit or damp spots underneath.
  • ✓ The temperature and pressure relief valve discharging repeatedly.
  • ✓ A pilot light or burner that will not stay lit.
  • ✓ Visible corrosion at the fittings, nipples, or tank seam.
  • ✓ A tank that is more than ten to twelve years old showing any of the above.

Our process and equipment

Not every water heater problem is a replacement. A failed element, thermocouple, or valve is a repair, and we check for that before quoting a new unit.

Diagnose before recommending

We test the actual failure point rather than assuming the tank is done. Elements, thermostats, thermocouples, gas valves, and relief valves are all repairable parts.

Sediment and hard water

Kansas City water leaves scale. Sediment at the bottom of a tank causes the rumbling most homeowners hear and shortens tank life. Flushing is often part of the fix.

Correct sizing on replacement

Replacement is sized to fuel type, household demand, recovery rate, and the space available, not just matched to whatever was there before.

Tank and tankless

We work on both. Tankless makes sense in some homes and not others, and it depends on gas supply, venting, and how the household actually uses hot water.

Venting, code, and permits

Combustion air, venting, expansion control, and permit requirements vary by jurisdiction across the metro. We confirm the requirements for your address before installation.

Haul-away and cleanup

The old unit leaves with us. We clean the area and walk you through the new unit's shutoff, temperature setting, and maintenance before we go.

Frequently asked questions

What affects the cost of water heater repair or replacement?

Whether it is a repair or a replacement is the biggest factor. A thermocouple or element is a small job. A full replacement depends on tank size, fuel type, whether you stay with the same configuration, and what the current venting and connections require to meet code. Tankless conversions cost more up front because they usually involve gas sizing and venting changes. We give you the options and the price before we start.

How quickly can you get to my house in the Kansas City metro?

We are a local crew rather than a national dispatch center, so the honest answer depends on where you are and what is already on the schedule. Closer-in areas like Downtown, Midtown, North Kansas City, and Raytown are usually quickest. Independence, Blue Springs, and Lee's Summit take longer, especially in traffic. Call and we will give you a real arrival window instead of a guess.

Do you warranty water heater work?

We offer workmanship and parts warranty options, and we work with home warranty programs. What applies depends on the job and the condition of the system, so we tell you which coverage fits before the work starts, not after.

Is a leaking water heater an emergency?

Yes. We answer the phone 24 hours a day, seven days a week, holidays included. Plumbing failures do not wait for business hours. If water or sewage is actively coming into the house, call now rather than waiting for morning.
