Plumbing in Lee's Summit

A city that mixes 1860s railroad blocks with modern subdivisions.

Lee's Summit grew up around the railroad in the 1860s, and Downtown Lee's Summit still has the older commercial blocks and neighborhoods to show for it. Since then the city has expanded steadily south and east, with subdivisions of larger homes, homeowner associations, and lakeside communities around Longview Lake and Lakewood. Unity Village is close by.

Those two halves of the city ask very different things of a plumber. Historic homes near downtown call for careful work on aging drain and supply lines. Newer homes tend to be bigger, with three or four bathrooms, finished basements, and hot water demand to match, so decisions about water heater size, expansion tanks, and gas capacity matter more than they would in a smaller house.

Larger lots also mean longer runs between the street and the house. A failed water service or sewer line can be a serious dig, and we plan those jobs around irrigation lines, mature landscaping, and any HOA requirements.

Tank and tankless water heaters for bigger households

When four or five people shower in the same morning, a standard 40-gallon tank falls behind. We size replacements to the household, install tankless units where the demand and gas supply support them, and set expansion tanks on closed plumbing systems so pressure spikes do not shorten the heater's life. If your current unit is on its way out, ask about stepping up to a better fit.

About water heater service
🔥

Gas lines for generators, grills, and outdoor kitchens

Standby generators, fire pits, outdoor kitchens, and pool heaters all pull gas that the original line was never sized for. We calculate the load, run new piping from the meter side of the house, pressure test it, and check every fitting for leaks. Every city sets its own permit and inspection requirements, so ask up front what your address needs. And if gas is ever in the air, get out and call 911 before anything else.

About gas line services

Excavation for long service and sewer lines

On larger Lee's Summit lots, the water service and sewer line can run a long way from the street. When a failed section has to be opened up, we get buried utilities marked through 811, plan the trench around irrigation and mature plantings, and dig no more than the repair demands. Rock and clay are common across the metro, so we set expectations on timing before the first shovel goes in.

Whole-home repipes for copper and older supply lines

Pinhole leaks in copper, low pressure, and stained water all point to supply piping at the end of its life. Homes in the older parts of town may have galvanized steel, and some copper systems in newer homes develop pinholes from water chemistry over time. We replace supply lines with PEX or copper depending on the house, and we tell you which material we recommend and why.

Smoke tests after remodels and basement finishing

Remodels and basement finishes sometimes leave vent tie-ins disconnected or cleanouts buried behind drywall, and the first sign is a faint sewer smell. A smoke test through the drain and vent system shows where the odor is getting out, so repairs stay local instead of opening walls at random. We also run smoke tests as a pre-purchase check on older homes near downtown.

Common questions

Questions Lee's Summit homeowners ask

What size water heater does a large home need?

It depends on how many people live there, how many bathrooms run at once, and whether you have large soaking tubs or multiple showerheads. We match capacity to real usage rather than the bare minimum, and we will talk through tank versus tankless for your gas and space situation.

Can you add a gas line for a generator or outdoor kitchen?

Yes. The first step is calculating the total gas load so the line is sized correctly for everything it will feed. Then we run and pressure test the new piping. Ask us up front about permits and inspections for your address.

Will excavation damage my landscaping?

Any trench affects the ground it crosses, but planning helps. We locate utilities, work around irrigation and plantings where we can, and talk through what to expect afterward. Raise restoration questions when you schedule the estimate so nothing surprises you.

Do you work in older homes near downtown Lee's Summit?

Yes. Older homes there often have galvanized supply lines, cast iron or clay drains, and unusual routing that has changed with each remodel. We inspect before recommending, so you get a repair plan that fits the actual house.
