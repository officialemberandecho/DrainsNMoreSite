Plumbing in Gladstone

Established Northland streets and the plumbing behind them.

Gladstone is a Clay County city in the Northland, with Antioch Road, North Oak Trafficway, and 76th Street as its main routes and Happy Rock Park, Linden Square, and the Gladstone Community Center as familiar landmarks. Much of the housing dates to the middle of the last century, when the Northland filled in with ranches, split-levels, and two-story homes.

Homes from that era have sturdy bones and aging systems. Copper supply lines that have been in service for decades can develop pinhole leaks. Gas piping to furnaces, water heaters, and ranges may be original iron. Cast iron drains have often been patched and extended over time. None of that is unusual, but it is exactly what to look for before a small leak or odor becomes an emergency.

Many Gladstone homes also have finished basements and long-unused floor drains, so sewer smells and slow leaks tend to show up in living space. We diagnose first and repair second, so the fix matches the actual problem.

Gas line repipes for furnaces, water heaters, and ranges

Original iron gas pipe in a mid-century home can corrode at fittings and joints. We replace worn sections or complete runs, pressure test the new piping, and check for leaks before we call the job done. If you are adding an appliance, we size the line to handle it. Permits and inspections vary by city, so we go over what your project needs. If you smell gas, get out and call 911.

Whole-home repipes for aging copper and galvanized pipe

Repeated pinhole leaks in copper, or falling pressure from galvanized pipe, mean the system is wearing out as a whole. Replacing sections one leak at a time adds up. We can price a full repipe with PEX or copper and compare it to targeted repairs, so you can choose based on what the house needs.

Smoke tests for basement sewer odor

Musty or sewer smells in a finished basement usually come from a dry trap, an open cleanout, or a crack in the drain or vent system. Smoke testing pinpoints which. We can also inspect roof vents and floor drains to confirm they are doing their jobs. Fixing the right thing the first time saves money.

Gladstone plumbing FAQ

Are pinhole leaks in copper a sign I need a repipe?

One pinhole is a repair. Several in a short time usually mean the pipe is failing from the inside. We can evaluate the pattern and give you both repair and repipe pricing.

How do I know if my gas line needs repiping?

Signs include a gas smell near appliances, corroded fittings, and piping that is too small for a newly added appliance. Never hunt for a leak with a flame. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911, then call us for the repair.

Why does my basement smell like sewage?

Most of the time it is a dry floor drain trap, an open cleanout, or a small crack in the drain or vent. Pouring water into an unused drain sometimes solves the first one. If the smell persists, a smoke test shows where it is getting in.
