What we do

Six core services, built for older Kansas City pipes and newer homes.

Our work falls into six categories: water heater repair and installation, toilet installation, smoke testing for sewer odors and vent leaks, plumbing excavation for buried water and sewer lines, gas line repipes, and whole-home repipes. Emergency drain cleaning and sewer line repair round out the emergency side of the business.

Each service page explains how the work is done, what affects the outcome, and the questions we hear most. Pick the one that matches your problem, or call and describe what you are seeing and we will point you the right way.

Common questions

Plumbing service questions

Which service should I call about a sewer smell?

Start with smoke testing. It shows where the drain and vent system is leaking into your home, which tells us whether the fix is a trap, a vent, or a line repair.

Can you install a toilet I bought myself?

Yes. We install the toilet you chose, check the flange, set it on a fresh seal, and test the fill and flush.

Do you handle emergencies outside business hours?

Yes. We are on call 24 hours a day, seven days a week, for backups, leaks, and other plumbing emergencies.
