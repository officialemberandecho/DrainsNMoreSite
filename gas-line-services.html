Common signs of a gas line problem

If you smell gas right now, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not flip switches, use a phone indoors, or look for the leak yourself. Once the property is safe, these are the signs that bring us out.

  • ✓ A rotten egg or sulfur smell near an appliance, the meter, or out in the yard.
  • ✓ A hissing or whistling sound near a gas line, valve, or connection.
  • ✓ Dead or discolored vegetation in a strip above a buried gas run.
  • ✓ A gas bill that climbed with no change in how much you are using.
  • ✓ Pilot lights that repeatedly blow out or refuse to stay lit.
  • ✓ A yellow or orange burner flame where it should be steady blue.
  • ✓ Soot or scorch marks around an appliance or its vent.
  • ✓ Corroded, painted-over, or physically damaged pipe and fittings.

Our process and equipment

Gas work is permitted, inspected work. The sequence below is not optional detail, it is what separates a legal installation from a liability.

Assessment and appliance load

We calculate the total demand of the appliances on the system. Undersized pipe is a common problem in homes where a range, dryer, or tankless unit was added later.

Material and routing

Material selection and routing account for run length, pressure drop, exposure, and access to shutoffs. Corrosion protection matters on anything buried or exposed to weather.

Pressure testing

The system is pressurized and monitored to confirm it holds. A test that holds is the documentation that the joints and connections are sound.

Permits and inspection

Permit and inspection requirements vary across Kansas City, Independence, Lee's Summit, and the surrounding jurisdictions. We confirm what your address requires before work starts.

Shutoff access

Every appliance connection needs an accessible shutoff. We show you where each one is and how to use it before we leave.

Documentation

You get the test results and the scope of what was installed, which matters for resale, insurance, and any future work on the system.

Frequently asked questions

What affects the cost of gas line work?

Run length, pipe size, material, and access. Adding a short branch to a nearby appliance is a small job. Running a new line across a finished basement, under a slab, or out to a detached garage is not. Permit and inspection fees vary by jurisdiction across the metro. If the existing system turns out to be undersized for the appliance load, that changes scope, and we tell you before starting rather than mid-job.

How quickly can you get to my house in the Kansas City metro?

We are a local crew rather than a national dispatch center, so the honest answer depends on where you are and what is already on the schedule. Closer-in areas like Downtown, Midtown, North Kansas City, and Raytown are usually quickest. Independence, Blue Springs, and Lee's Summit take longer, especially in traffic. Call and we will give you a real arrival window instead of a guess.

Do you warranty gas piping work?

We offer workmanship and parts warranty options, and we work with home warranty programs. What applies depends on the job and the condition of the system, so we tell you which coverage fits before the work starts, not after.

What should I do if I smell gas right now?

Leave the building immediately and take everyone with you. Do not turn switches or appliances on or off, and do not use a phone until you are outside and away from the structure. Call the gas utility or 911 first. Once they have made the property safe, call us and we will handle the piping repair. We answer 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
