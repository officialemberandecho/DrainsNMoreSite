Smoke testing for sewer gas leak detection

Persistent sewer odor can come from a dry trap, damaged vent, disconnected drain, or hidden opening. A controlled smoke test can help trace where smoke escapes so the repair targets the actual entry point.

Trace the source

Smoke can reveal openings around drains, vents, cleanouts, and plumbing connections that are difficult to find by odor alone.

Protect the home

We consider occupied spaces, ventilation, fixtures, and the condition of the drainage system before testing.

Repair the cause

Results may point to a trap, vent, drain connection, foundation drain, or sewer lateral issue that needs a separate repair plan.
