Common signs you need emergency drain cleaning

One slow sink is usually a fixture problem. Several fixtures acting up at the same time almost always means the main line. These are the symptoms worth calling about instead of reaching for another bottle of drain chemical.

  • ✓ Two or more fixtures back up at once, such as a tub and a toilet, or a toilet and a floor drain.
  • ✓ The toilet gurgles or bubbles when the washing machine drains.
  • ✓ Water rises in the basement floor drain while someone showers upstairs.
  • ✓ Sewage odor near a floor drain, crawlspace, or laundry area.
  • ✓ The same fixture clogs again within days of being plunged or treated.
  • ✓ A tub or shower drains a little slower every week.
  • ✓ The lowest fixture in the house backs up first.
  • ✓ Water comes up in one fixture when you run a different one.

Our process and equipment

Clearing a drain without knowing what caused the blockage is how the same line backs up a month later. We work in this order every time.

Locate the problem first

We find the cleanout and determine whether the blockage is in a single branch line or the main. Clearing the wrong line costs you money and tells you nothing about the real cause.

Cable machine for roots and solids

A drain machine with the right cutter head handles root intrusion and hard obstructions in the cast iron and clay lines common in older Kansas City neighborhoods.

Hydro-jetting for grease and scale

High-pressure water scours the pipe wall instead of punching a hole through the middle of a blockage. We use it when the pipe material and condition can safely take the pressure, which is exactly why we inspect before jetting.

Camera inspection on repeat clogs

If a line has backed up more than once, we put a camera in it. Repeat backups are usually a belly, a break, or root intrusion, not bad luck.

Containment and cleanup

Floors get covered and water gets contained before the machine runs. We clean the work area before we leave.

Verify flow before we go

We run the fixture long enough to confirm the line is actually moving water, and tell you what we saw and what we recommend next.

Frequently asked questions

What affects the cost of emergency drain cleaning?

Access, method, and severity. A line with an accessible cleanout costs less to clear than one that requires pulling a toilet or opening a wall. Cabling a single branch line is less involved than jetting a main. Whether a camera inspection is needed to find the cause also matters. We look at the situation, walk you through the options, and give you the price before any work begins.

How quickly can you get to my house in the Kansas City metro?

We are a local crew rather than a national dispatch center, so the honest answer depends on where you are and what is already on the schedule. Closer-in areas like Downtown, Midtown, North Kansas City, and Raytown are usually quickest. Independence, Blue Springs, and Lee's Summit take longer, especially in traffic. Call and we will give you a real arrival window instead of a guess.

Do you guarantee drain cleaning work?

We offer workmanship and parts warranty options, and we work with home warranty programs. What applies depends on the job and the condition of the system, so we tell you which coverage fits before the work starts, not after.

Are you really available nights and weekends?

Yes. We answer the phone 24 hours a day, seven days a week, holidays included. Plumbing failures do not wait for business hours. If water or sewage is actively coming into the house, call now rather than waiting for morning.
